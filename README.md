## 👋 About us

SolutionX is a software development company based in Székesfehérvár, Hungary, specialising in payroll and HR. We combine AI with modern software engineering to give our clients more efficient HR processes, smarter decision support and faster business systems.

## 🧩 What we build

- **ORION HR Platform**: a unified system for HR, labour administration and payroll, available on web and mobile
- **SX Bér**: AI-assisted payroll software, always up to date with Hungarian regulations
- **Digitalisation & automation**: custom integrations and process automation that cut down on administrative work
